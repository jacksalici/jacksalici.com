I am interested in AI once it leaves the screen: social robots that need to hold a conversation or strike a pose, and vehicles and infrastructure that need to sense, communicate, and act in real time. This activity spans human-robot interaction, LLM-driven motion and pose generation, and cooperative, connected vehicles, from itinerary optimization to inter-vehicular communication.
