A good part of my research looks at deep learning models as reusable, composable parts rather than monoliths trained from scratch: merging, transporting, and adapting knowledge across pre-trained models. The same modular mindset carries into the computer vision and multimodal pipelines I build around it, for tasks like gaze and emotion analysis or multimodal medical data.
